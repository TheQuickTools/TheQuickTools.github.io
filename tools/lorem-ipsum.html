What Is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is a scrambled passage of Latin text derived from a work by Cicero, dating back to 45 BC. It has been used as placeholder text in typesetting since the 1500s because it roughly resembles natural language distribution, making it look like readable English without actually conveying meaning. Designers and developers use it to demonstrate layouts, fonts, and spacing before real content is available.
