Why Word Count Matters

Word count is a critical metric across many fields. SEO experts track it to ensure content meets minimum thresholds for search engine ranking. Students and academics must meet essay and thesis word limits. Authors and journalists use word counts to manage article length and pacing. Social media managers monitor character and word counts to fit platform constraints. This tool provides all the metrics you need in one place, updated in real time as you type.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is reading time calculated?Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute, which is the average adult reading speed for non-fiction content. Speaking time uses 130 WPM, a common pace for presentations.
What counts as a word?A word is any sequence of characters separated by whitespace. Contractions like "don't" count as one word, and hyphenated terms count as one word unless separated by spaces.
Is my text stored on a server?No. All counting happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never transmitted, stored, or accessible to anyone else.
