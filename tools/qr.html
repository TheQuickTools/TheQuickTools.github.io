About QR Codes

QR codes (Quick Response codes) were originally designed for the automotive industry but have become ubiquitous in everyday life. They store data in a grid of black and white squares that any smartphone camera can scan. QR codes are used for linking to websites, sharing contact details, initiating payments, and providing product information. They can hold up to 4,296 alphanumeric characters, making them versatile for a wide range of use cases.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much data can a QR code hold?A QR code can store up to 4,296 alphanumeric characters, 7,089 numeric digits, or 2,953 bytes of binary data, depending on the error correction level.
Can QR codes be scanned without an app?Yes. Modern smartphone cameras on iOS and Android can natively scan QR codes — just point your camera at the code and a notification will appear.
What are common uses for QR codes?QR codes are used for website URLs, Wi-Fi password sharing, restaurant menus, event tickets, payment processing, business cards, and product packaging.
