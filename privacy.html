Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

QuickTools is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our website and tools.

Data Collection

We do not collect personal data. All tools on QuickTools run entirely in your browser. No data you enter into our tools is ever sent to our servers or stored anywhere.

When you use a calculator, converter, or any other tool, the computation happens locally on your device. We have no access to your inputs, results, or usage patterns within the tools.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

Google AdSenseDisplays advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings.
Google AnalyticsMay be used to understand site traffic patterns. Analytics data is aggregated and anonymous.
Google FontsLoads fonts from Google's CDN for better typography.

Cookies

QuickTools itself does not set any cookies. Third-party services (Google AdSense, Google Analytics) may set cookies for advertising and analytics purposes. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Children's Privacy

QuickTools does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Our tools are general-purpose utilities available to everyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via GitHub.
